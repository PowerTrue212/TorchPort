This directory is a self-contained copy prepared for review and commit. It does
not replace or move any existing repository file.

- RAFT protocol, prompts, evaluator/report scripts, templates, and tests.
- Sanitized three-method evidence bundle.
- Final figures and their plotting data/scripts.

`experiments/results/raft-v1-2026-08-25-v100/manifest.json`.
